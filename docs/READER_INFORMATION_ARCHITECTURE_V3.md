## Reader navigation

`Home | Read | World | Codex | Fan Page | Manga | Support`

The navigation is centered. Every item is its own page.

## Home

Approved GENESIS key art only, plus persistent navigation. No stacked homepage panels.

## Read

Released Episodes and novel reader. Part comments appear beneath the selected accessible Part. Advance-access comments remain hidden from readers who cannot access that Part.

## World

Reader-safe previews:
- Maps
- Monsters
- Equipment

## Codex

Searchable release-gated reader database.

## Fan Page

Reader and Admin image/community posts with captions and comments. Reader uploads require account + moderation. Private media paths are never public.

## Manga

Dedicated page with **COMING SOON** only. No invented chapters or schedule.

## Support

Rewarded:
- ₱10 = 1 Advance Part
- ₱50 = 15 Advance Parts
- normal hard horizon = 21 Parts / 1 week
- VIP ₱200 = 30-day VIP, up to 90 Parts / 1 month ahead
- rewarded paid cap = ₱200 per reader per rolling 30 days

Pure support:
- any amount
- no credits
- no VIP
- no extra Parts
- no return expected
- not counted toward rewarded-support cap

Share reward:
- 1 verified share = 1 Advance Part
- max 1/day
- max 5/week
- still capped by normal 21-Part horizon

Recognition:
- optional Supporter badge
- optional VIP badge while VIP is active
- never show payment amount

Contact Us:
- authenticated reader → private Admin conversation
- Admin Control Center → Messages inbox

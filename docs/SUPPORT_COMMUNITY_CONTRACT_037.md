Authority: **037**

## Non-negotiable access boundaries

Support can only move a reader into **release-ready scheduled Final Canon**.

Never unlock:
- AI2 draft
- AI1 working text
- hidden roadmap prose
- unscheduled manuscript
- failed QA/finalization output

## Rewarded paid ceiling

The absolute rewarded-support spending ceiling is **₱200 per reader per rolling 30 days**.

Pure no-return support is not a rewarded product and is outside that ceiling, but grants **no reading benefit**.

## Normal advance

Maximum **21 scheduled Parts ahead**.

## VIP advance

₱200 VIP, active for 30 days, maximum **90 scheduled Parts ahead**.

## Community

Comments and Fan Page content inherit the reader's Part-access boundary. Advance spoilers cannot leak to readers without access.

## Public badges

Supporter/VIP badges are opt-in. They show recognition only. Never expose amount donated or paid.

## Pre-launch

No real money collection until an approved payment provider, webhook verification, idempotency, refund handling, and end-to-end tests are in place.

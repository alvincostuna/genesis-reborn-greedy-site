E003-P03 — A Quest With No Mystery

The Gnawer launched again.

Flavio moved before the creature left the ground.

Not far.

Half a step to the outside, just enough to let the body pass where his leg had been.

The beginner sword came around after it.

Too fast.

Flavio felt the angle go wrong and stopped the strike rather than force it.

The opening disappeared.

Nico made a sound of personal disappointment.

“You stopped!”

“Yes.”

“You had already dodged it.”

“I dodged it. That does not make the next swing correct.”

“That is an extremely responsible sentence for someone holding a sword.”

Flavio kept his eyes on the grass.

The Gnawer had landed and slipped into cover.

His thigh still hurt from the first hit.

The pain was not severe, but it changed the way he stood. He shortened his stance and shifted more weight toward the uninjured leg.

Nico noticed.

“You want me to take it?”

Flavio considered the offer.

That mattered.

He actually considered it.

“No. If it gets worse, yes.”

Nico nodded.

No joke.

Good.

The Gnawer surfaced several meters away.

Flavio watched.

The creature feinted left.

He did not chase.

Its shoulders dipped.

He waited.

Rear legs gathered.

There.

The launch came.

Flavio shifted.

The Gnawer passed.

He turned with it and waited until the safe angle appeared before committing.

The sword struck cleanly.

The creature hit the ground.

This time, it did not rise.

A pale official notification appeared.

[VERGE GNAWER DEFEATED]

Flavio stared.

The body remained for several seconds before beginning to fade.

Nico raised both arms.

“Yes!”

Flavio exhaled.

“Now?”

“What?”

“Now you can celebrate.”

Nico shouted louder.

Flavio laughed.

Then another window appeared.

[EXP GAINED]

10 EXP

Flavio stopped laughing.

Ten.

He looked at the number.

Then opened his status.

Level 1.

EXP: 10 / 400

Flavio stared.

Four hundred needed.

Ten received.

His eyes moved from one number to the other.

Nico leaned closer.

“What?”

Flavio pointed.

“Ten.”

“Yes.”

“Four hundred.”

“Yes.”

“Ten.”

Nico blinked.

“I can also read.”

Flavio looked toward the grass.

Three monsters for the quest.

Ten EXP each, if the reward remained consistent.

Thirty from kills.

Sixty from completion.

Ninety total.

Still not enough for a level.

Good.

Nico was watching him.

“Why are you smiling?”

“I’m not.”

“You are.”

Flavio looked at the field.

“How many Gnawers are out there?”

Nico’s face changed.

“No.”

“I asked a question.”

“I recognize that face now.”

“What face?”

“The face you make when numbers have become a problem you want to personally defeat.”

Flavio sheathed the sword for a moment.

His thigh needed a break.

He was not going to grind injured.

Not yet.

That last thought arrived too naturally.

Flavio ignored it.

The defeated Gnawer finished fading.

Something remained in the grass.

A small loot marker.

Nico pointed.

“Drop.”

Flavio crouched.

The official interface identified the items.

[LOOT]

Gnawer Incisor

Rough Hide Scrap

1 Copper

Flavio looked at the teeth.

Nico looked at Flavio.

“No.”

“What?”

“You are doing it again.”

“Doing what?”

“Looking at garbage like it is inventory.”

“It is inventory.”

“It is a tooth.”

“It has a system label.”

“It is still a tooth.”

Flavio collected the items.

The satchel registered them.

He inspected the rough hide.

No obvious value shown beyond the item name.

That meant nothing.

Worth could come from vendors, crafting, quests, or players.

Nico sighed.

“We killed one rat and you are already evaluating the corpse.”

“Gnawer.”

“It is a fantasy rat.”

“Fantasy rats may have markets.”

“That sentence is why you should not be allowed near an auction house.”

Flavio stood.

His leg protested.

He looked at the HP bar.

Damage remained.

“Rest.”

Nico looked surprised.

“What?”

“We rest.”

“You’re saying that voluntarily?”

“Yes.”

“Inside a game?”

“Yes.”

Nico looked around dramatically.

“Genesis really is miraculous.”

They moved back toward the road and sat near a marker stone where other players passed regularly.

Flavio drank from the starter water supply.

The game restored nothing instantly from the action.

Good.

Nico leaned back on his hands.

“So?”

“So what?”

“First fight.”

Flavio looked at the field.

He thought about the first hit.

The wrong cue.

The second dodge.

The poor swing he stopped.

The clean strike.

The ten EXP.

“It hurts.”

Nico laughed.

“That is your review?”

“It also moves faster than it looks.”

“Better.”

“The pause is not the cue.”

Nico nodded.

“The legs.”

“Maybe.”

“You saw it twice.”

“Twice is not enough for a universal rule.”

Nico groaned.

Flavio smiled.

He took out the Gnawer Incisor from inventory, inspected it again, then put it back.

No hidden description appeared.

No private interface.

No clue.

Just loot.

He liked that more than he expected.

The game was not giving him special answers.

He would have to learn the same way everyone else did.

Maybe faster.

Maybe not.

But honestly.

His HP recovered slowly while they rested.

Nico checked his own quest.

“Two more.”

“Yes.”

“You want the next one?”

“We both need practice.”

Nico blinked.

That answer had surprised him.

Flavio noticed.

“What?”

“I thought you would say yes.”

“I did the first.”

“That has never stopped you from taking more work.”

Flavio looked at him.

“This is not work.”

Nico laughed.

“Give it ten minutes.”

Flavio did not answer.

They found the second Gnawer farther from the gate.

This time Nico took the front.

His style was different.

Faster.

Less patient.

He reacted well but chased openings too eagerly.

The Gnawer made him pay for it with a bite on the forearm.

Nico shouted something unprintable.

Flavio stepped in.

“Back.”

“I’m fine.”

Flavio looked at him.

Nico stopped.

Then laughed.

“Right. I hate when you use my words.”

They reset.

This time, they worked together.

Nico drew the movement.

Flavio watched the angle.

Nico avoided the first lunge.

Flavio struck after the creature landed.

Not enough.

The Gnawer turned.

Nico hit from the other side.

The monster fell.

[VERGE GNAWER DEFEATED]

[EXP GAINED]

Flavio checked.

Another ten.

Nico opened his status too.

“Same.”

The loot differed.

No incisor.

A small hide scrap.

Two copper.

Flavio looked at it.

Nico pointed at him.

“Do not start.”

“I said nothing.”

“Your eyes did.”

The third target took longer to find.

By then, the field had become crowded.

Players moved deeper.

Spawned Gnawers disappeared quickly.

Flavio understood why the marsh quest paid more.

Time was a cost.

Competition was another.

He filed that away without narrating a lesson to himself.

He simply remembered.

The third Gnawer appeared near a shallow ditch.

This fight went better.

Not perfectly.

Better.

Flavio and Nico did not talk much during it.

That was progress.

Nico drew the first rush.

Flavio moved to the side.

The creature changed direction.

Nico nearly chased.

Stopped.

Good.

The Gnawer lunged.

Flavio struck after the landing.

Nico followed.

The monster died before either took damage.

Quest progress updated.

3 / 3

The official window appeared.

[QUEST OBJECTIVE COMPLETE]

Return to the request board.

Nico raised his sword.

“Three!”

Flavio looked at his status.

Thirty EXP from kills.

No hidden bonus.

No special notification.

Perfect.

Nico saw the expression.

“You wanted more.”

“I wanted information.”

“You always say that when you wanted more.”

Flavio sheathed the sword.

They walked back toward Hiraya.

The city gate looked different after the field.

Not safer, exactly.

More familiar.

Flavio’s leg still ached faintly.

His hands remembered the sword.

His inventory now contained ugly little things he had earned by killing ugly little monsters.

It felt ridiculous.

It also felt real.

At the request board, Flavio completed the quest.

[QUEST COMPLETE]

Outer Verge Pest Clearing

Reward:
60 EXP
8 Copper

[EXP GAINED]

60 EXP

[CURRENCY RECEIVED]

8 Copper

Flavio opened his status.

EXP: 90 / 400

Nico looked too.

“Ninety.”

Flavio nodded.

Nico followed his gaze.

Then sighed.

“How many Gnawers are you thinking about?”

Flavio looked toward the eastern gate.

“Enough.”

“That is not a number.”

“It can become one.”

Nico groaned.

Flavio smiled.

The quest had contained no mystery.

Three monsters.

One field.

One fixed reward.

That was exactly why Flavio liked it.

The world had finally given him something simple enough to measure.

The dangerous part was how much he enjoyed measuring it.
